Principal investigator

Umberto Spagnolini

Full Professor of Telecommunications

Umberto Spagnolini has been a Full Professor of Telecommunications at Politecnico di Milano since 2006. His research focuses on signal processing for mobile communications, radar and remote sensing. He previously served as an Assistant Professor and an Associate Professor at the university.
